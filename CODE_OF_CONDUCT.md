# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Journey of Life (JOL) platform serves religious institutions and their communities across the
European Union. Everyone who contributes to, reviews, or governs this policy suite is expected to
act with integrity, professionalism, and respect — consistent with the ethical obligations that
underpin our Information Security Management System (SOC 2 CC1.1) and applicable EU law.

## Standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Act honestly and with professional integrity, including in the reporting of security and
  compliance findings.
- Treat all participants respectfully; harassment, discrimination, and abusive behaviour are not
  tolerated.
- Respect confidentiality: never publish personal data, customer/tenant identifiers, credentials,
  or other sensitive information in issues, pull requests, or policy text.
- Provide and receive constructive, actionable feedback.
- Escalate genuine disagreements about policy or compliance through the documented change and
  exception processes rather than circumventing them.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorisation.
- Committing real secrets, credentials, or personal data to this public repository.
- Knowingly introducing content that misrepresents JOL's compliance posture.

## Reporting

Report concerns about conduct through a private channel:

- **Email:** `conduct@journeyoflife.org`
- **Security or compliance concerns:** see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Reports are handled by the policy owner and the JOL Governance Board. All reports are treated
confidentially to the extent permitted by law.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful. Enforcement decisions follow the principles of proportionality, due process, and
documentation, and are recorded where a compliance impact exists.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct supports the ethical and governance obligations referenced by
`POL-ISM-001` and `POL-AUP-005` and is maintained by the JOL Governance Board.
